# Pipeline Data Dictionary

This document outlines the schema, origins, and calculation methodologies for the data tables generated by the `exchange_analysis` pipeline. Tables are categorized by their logical table names (passed to the I/O handler) rather than physical file names.

---

## 1. Raw Data Tables
These tables contain the direct, unpatched arrays retrieved from the ENTSO-E API or Elexon BMRS.

### `raw_generation` / `raw_load`
* **Origin:** ENTSO-E / Elexon BMRS
* **Description:** Raw, unresampled generation (per technology) and demand arrays.
* **Columns:**
  * `[Generation Type]`: Raw generation MW per technology (e.g., `Wind Onshore`, `Nuclear`).
  * `Actual Load` / `Load`: Raw consumption MW.

### `raw_commercial_flows` / `raw_commercial_flows_da` / `raw_physical_flows`
* **Origin:** ENTSO-E
* **Description:** Raw cross-border exchange schedules (total or day-ahead) and physical grid flows.
* **Columns:**
  * `[bz]_[n]`: Outward flow from the target zone to a specific neighbor (e.g., `DE_FR`).
  * `[n]_[bz]`: Inward flow from a specific neighbor to the target zone (e.g., `FR_DE`).

### `raw_market_price_dayahead` / `raw_net_positions_dayahead`
* **Origin:** ENTSO-E
* **Description:** Baseline market metrics, resampled to 1h averages.
* **Columns:**
  * `Value`: The specific metric value in EUR/MWh (price) or MW (net position). *(Note: IT net positions for 2025 are inverted to fix ENTSO-E sign convention errors).*

## 2. Processed & Synchronized Tables
These tables have undergone 1h resampling, gap-filling imputation, negative-clipping, and structural balancing.

### `processed_generation`
* **Description:** Cleaned generation and load data, augmented with physical net positions.
* **Columns:**
  * `[Generation Type]`: Cleaned generation data (clipped to ≥ 0).
  * `Storage Charge`: Absolute sum of negative storage generation (grid consumption by storage).
  * `Storage Discharge`: Sum of positive storage generation (grid injection by storage).
  * `Generation`: Sum of all active generation components (excluding storage).
  * `Total Generation`: `Generation` + `Storage Discharge`.
  * `Demand`: Cleaned load data.
  * `Total Load`: `Demand` + `Storage Charge`.
  * `Net Export`: `Total Generation` - `Total Load`.
  * `gap_filling_method`: Audit string tracking imputation methods and negative clippings.
  * `download_timestamp`: Temporal vintage of the dataset.

### `processed_commercial_flows` / `processed_commercial_flows_da` / `processed_physical_flows`
* **Description:** Cleaned bilateral flow matrices with resolved reporting symmetries.
* **Columns:**
  * `[bz]_[n]` / `[n]_[bz]`: Cleaned, symmetry-enforced directional border flows.
  * `[bz]_[n]_net_export`: The net flow for a specific border (`[bz]_[n]` - `[n]_[bz]`).
  * `Net Export`: Sum of all individual border net exports.
  * `gap_filling_method`: Audit string tracking imputation and symmetry conflict resolutions (e.g., `TRUSTED_TIEBREAKER`).

## 3. Hourly Analysis & Decomposition Tables
These tables represent the high-granularity (1h) results of the analysis script. Because the script generates dozens of permutations, they are categorized by their **table suffix** across all methodologies (`analysis_cft_*`, `tracing_agg_coupling_*`, `tracing_direct_coupling_*`, `pool_*`).

### Level 1: Source Zone Breakdown (`*_bz`)
* **Tables:** `analysis_cft_total_bz`, `tracing_agg_coupling_bz`, `pool_physical_net_pos_bz`, etc.
* **Description:** Shows how much volume is imported from each source bidding zone.
* **Columns:**
  * `[n]` (e.g., `FR`, `NL`): The hourly MW volume imported into the target zone from zone `n`.

### Level 2: Granular Decomposition (`*_type_bz`)
* **Tables:** `analysis_cft_total_type_bz`, `tracing_agg_coupling_type_bz`, `pool_commercial_net_pos_type_bz`, etc.
* **Description:** Breaks down the imported volume from specific zones into the constituent technologies.
* **Columns:**
  * `[n]_[Type]` (e.g., `FR_Nuclear`): The imported volume from zone `n` multiplied by zone `n`'s internal generation mix fraction for `[Type]`.

### Level 3: Technology Aggregation (`*_type`)
* **Tables:** `analysis_cft_total_type`, `tracing_agg_coupling_type`, `pool_physical_net_pos_type`, etc.
* **Description:** Total incoming volume by specific generation technology, regardless of geographic source.
* **Columns:**
  * `[Type]` (e.g., `Wind Onshore`, `Nuclear`): The sum of all incoming flows of this specific technology across all source zones.

### Level 4: Category Aggregation (`*_agg`)
* **Tables:** `analysis_cft_total_agg`, `tracing_direct_coupling_agg`, `pool_commercial_link_based_agg`, etc.
* **Description:** Total incoming volume mapped to high-level categories based on the `agg_map`.
* **Columns:**
  * `[Category]` (e.g., `Fossil`, `Renewable`): Sum of specific technology flows mapped to broader industry categories.

---

## 4. Post-Processed Annual Totals
These tables aggregate the high-granularity MW arrays into annualized Terawatt-hours (TWh) to allow for direct comparison of different accounting methodologies.

### `annual_imp_bz` / `annual_exp_bz` / `annual_imp_type` / `annual_exp_type` / `annual_imp_agg`
* **Description:** Annualized TWh totals for imports/exports broken down by zone, type, or category.
* **Structure:**
  * **Rows (Index):** The specific Bidding Zone (`FR`), Technology Type (`Nuclear`), or Category (`Renewable`).
  * **Columns:** The applied tracking methodology:
    * `CFT` (Commercial Flow Total)
    * `Netted CFT`
    * `Pooled Net CFT`
    * `Pooled Net Phys.`
    * `DC Flow Tracing` (Direct Coupling)
    * `AC Flow Tracing` (Aggregated Coupling)
